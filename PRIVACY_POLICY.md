# Privacy Policy for HTML Viewer & Editor

**Last updated: August 27, 2026**

## Overview

HTML Viewer & Editor is a completely offline application. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data — which is to say, we don't.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.** This app does not:
- Collect personally identifiable information
- Use analytics tools or SDKs
- Track your usage
- Send data to any server
- Require you to create an account
- Access the internet

## Data Storage

All HTML files you create are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage directory. This data:
- Is accessible only by this app
- Cannot be accessed by other apps
- Is automatically removed when you uninstall the app
- Is never transmitted off your device

## Permissions

This app requires **no permissions**. It does not access:
- Your camera
- Your contacts
- Your location
- Your microphone
- The internet
- External storage (beyond the app's own private storage)

## Third-Party Services

This app does **not** use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising networks.

## Children's Privacy

This app is safe for all ages. No data is collected from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

Since this app collects no data, this policy is unlikely to change. Any updates will be posted here.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact: support@example.com
